Welcome to SOCCSKSARGEN ! Explore breathtaking destinations, exciting activities, and rich culture.

SOCCSKSARGEN, officially Region XII, is a vibrant area in south‑central Mindanao composed of Cotabato, South Cotabato, Sultan Kudarat, Sarangani, and General Santos City. Known for its mix of mountains, valleys, and coastlines, the region is shaped by the Rio Grande de Mindanao, the island’s longest river. Its more than 4 million residents represent diverse groups such as the B’laan, T’boli, Manobo, Maguindanao, and Teduray, along with Christian settlers from Luzon and the Visayas. Once part of Central Mindanao, the region evolved into its current form, with Koronadal as the regional center and General Santos as its commercial hub. SOCCSKSARGEN today stands as a dynamic crossroads of culture, agriculture, and trade in Mindanao.
